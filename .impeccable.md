## Design Context

### Users
- **Restaurateurs / Owners / Managers**: Configuring system settings, fiscal tickets, brand colors, and logo. They need a simple, self-explanatory, and visually appealing settings dashboard.
- **Waiters / Chefs**: Using the POS / KDS systems. The branding (colors, logo) configured here will propagate to these screens, affecting readability, contrast, and visual identity.

### Brand Personality
- **Modern & Professional**: Polished, high-end feel for restaurant management.
- **Responsive & Dynamic**: Adapts fully to the configured brand colors.
- **Clear & Utilitarian**: High contrast, crisp typography, and visual hierarchy that works in fast-paced restaurant environments.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **V2 Aesthetic Dark Mode**: Dark neutral backgrounds tinted dynamically by the brand's primary color.
- **Accents**: Smooth gradients between primary and secondary brand colors.
- **Interactions**: Interactive transitions, custom inputs (for color picker and logo upload), responsive layouts.

### Design Principles
1. **Dynamic Cohesion**: The settings UI itself must adapt to and preview the chosen branding colors, rather than being hardcoded to blue.
2. **Visual Affordance**: Interactive elements (like upload zones and color inputs) must feel intuitive, responsive to hover/focus, and premium.
3. **No Placeholders / Full State Integrity**: Empty states, previews, error and success messages must be meticulously styled and match the theme.
